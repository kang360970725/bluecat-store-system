## In progress: operations dashboard

- The store-center/workspace forecast consistency fix was deployed to production on 2026-11-09. Production diagnosis confirmed that the store center projected the current month from eight complete days through November 8, while the workspace divided by nine days and included November 9's incomplete revenue. This understated workspace forecast profit by CNY 16k-27k in the largest observed cases. Both paths now share one tested rule and cent-level rounding: current-month projections use only revenue and elapsed days through yesterday; historical months use full-month actuals; each cost line is rounded to cents before aggregation. Post-deploy reconciliation returned zero revenue, elapsed-day and forecast-profit difference across all 17 ranking records. Store-center month selection is also carried in the workspace route, updated when the workspace month changes, and preserved when returning to the center.
